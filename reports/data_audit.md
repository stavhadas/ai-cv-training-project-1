**The coarser key that *is* recoverable with no assumptions: `(board, package)`.** Every image in one (board, package) cell is provably one physical batch — section 9's cell table gives its size directly, ranging from 10 images (e.g. `CS2/R0603`) to 34 (`CS6/R0805`) per viewpoint folder, i.e. roughly 10–34 *components* per cell once you count across viewpoints correctly. It costs granularity (a whole board/package sold together to one split) but requires no guess about capture order or ordinal alignment. This is the key to fall back to if Stage 1's perceptual-hash clustering (the standard fallback for "not recoverable" component ID) turns out to be unreliable.
